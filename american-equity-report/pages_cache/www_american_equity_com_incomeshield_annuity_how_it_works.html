American Equity IncomeShield BONUS 10

Jump start retirement savings with premium bonuses

Get premium bonuses to help give you a head start on your retirement savings. A 14% bonus is applied to all first-year premium payments and vests over a predetermined period.

Each year after the first contract year, a percentage is vested until 100% vested at the end of the contract. The vested amount of the premium bonus is guaranteed and cannot be forfeited as a result of a free withdrawal. In the event of a partial withdrawal or a surrender, premium bonus payouts will be based on the vesting schedule.

Example scenario

Jodi’s story

Jodi, age 55, worked with her financial planner to discover that her existing Social Security benefits will cover about 40% of her income needs. To help supplement her income, she purchased an American Equity IncomeShield fixed index annuity with a premium of $100,000. For additional flexibility, she added a Lifetime Income Benefit Rider with Wellbeing Benefit that can provide access to enhanced monthly payouts to help offset the burden of healthcare expenses.

Boosting income base - A 14% bonus is added to Jodi’s IAV on all first-year premiums. She can begin drawing income payments, but chooses to let her assets build.

Generating lifetime income - The LIBR with Wellbeing Benefit allows her to save income wth the interest credited to her IAV.

Drawing income - Jodi can build her benefit base over time. When she elects to begin drawing income, her payout factor is a percentage of the IAV based on her age. This income payment is guaranteed for life.

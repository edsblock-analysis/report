Driven by our values

We don’t just talk about our EPIC values, we hire for them, develop through them, and celebrate them. They guide both our internal culture and external commitments, from career development to community involvement.

Empowered

Every team member is empowered to make an impact through collaboration, inclusivity, and a commitment to shared success. 

Passionate

We bring passion, energy, and enthusiasm to everything we do, driving us to aim for excellence and deliver our best effort every day. 

Integrity

Our culture of trust is based on integrity built through honesty, accountability, and doing what is right for each other, even when it is difficult. 

Caring

Our people work, lead, and give back to our communities with care, compassion, respect, and real support that makes a difference.

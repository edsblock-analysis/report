Who we are

Our people. Our history. Our service.

After 50 years in the insurance industry, David Noble noticed a change in how companies operated — they had lost touch with their customers. So, he founded a new company focused on two crucial components: great service and great products. Guided by those foundational principles, American Equity grew into a leading annuities carrier.

We believe confidence starts with clarity. We offer annuity products that prioritize guarantees and long-term stability so that financial professionals can help clients approach retirement with confidence.

Headquartered in Des Moines, IA for more than three decades, we help our customers enjoy financial security and peace of mind by funding more than half a million retirements nationwide with our top-tier annuities and exceptional service.

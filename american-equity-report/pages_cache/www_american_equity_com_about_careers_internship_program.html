Why intern with American Equity?

A place to make an impact

We’re searching for students who are curious, driven, and eager to make a meaningful impact. Whether your focus is finance, IT, marketing, communications, or somewhere else, we welcome individuals who are eager to learn, collaborate, and bring a fresh perspective to the work we do while experiencing what it means to build a career.

Become a summer intern

Enjoy an immersive learning environment in which you’ll experience a variety of activities with your peers, mentors, and American Equity professionals.

Project work

Earn hands-on experience aligned to career goals, do group work on our current business challenges, and present recommendation proposals to leadership.

Training & development

Attend weekly business presentations and lunch and learn sessions to deepen your understanding of our industry and strategy while building professional skills. 

Networking

Experience a variety of valuable networking opportunities including volunteering and social events with peers through YURTern and DSM Intern Connection. 

Leadership support

Get direct support, feedback, and one-on-one communication with your department manager, mentors, and members of the Internship Program leadership team. 

A day in the life as an intern

No two days wil be the same — your internship will offer a variety of immersive learning experiences with your peers and American Equity leaders.

Connect directly with leaders from inside and outside the organization to build skills and practical insights on navigating the professional world. 

  • Candid learning from mentors and executives
  • Insights into the industry and company strategy
  • Professional educational programs

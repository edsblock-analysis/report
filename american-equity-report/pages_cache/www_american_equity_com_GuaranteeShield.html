American Equity GuaranteeShield

Guaranteed growth without market risk

Get fixed interest rates for 3- and 5-year terms, giving you dependable growth without the ups and downs of the market. Whether you’re planning for an upcoming retirement need or just looking for short-term certainty, this fixed annuity provides a secure way to grow your money with zero market risk — your rate is locked in from day one, allowing you to plan with confidence. 

Example scenario

Sam’s story

At 58 and with retirement getting closer, Sam had built a solid savings foundation but was looking for a way to protect a portion of it from market swings. He wanted peace of mind, a predictable rate of return, and the ability to access funds if needed. 


Sam chose American Equity GuaranteeShield 5 to protect his principal from market volatility while his interest compounded tax-deferred, boosting overall growth. The annuity also allowed a penalty-free withdrawal of up to 10% annually starting in year two, and ensured his beneficiaries would receive the full contract value if something happened to him.

Sam withdrew 5% of the contract value without penalty to cover an unexpected medical bill.

 

The annuity continued growing steadily, without stress or guesswork.

 

Confident in the predictable growth of his savings, Sam renewed the contract for another term.

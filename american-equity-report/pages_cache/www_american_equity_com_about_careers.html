Benefits to support your life and career

Our benefits package is an extension of our values and designed to support a healthy work-life balance. 

Medical

Comprehensive coverage starting day one for your peace of mind:

  • Health care plans with low premiums and deductibles
  • Free telehealth services
  • HSA with employer contribution
  • Dental and vision plans

Financial

Tools and protection to help plan and grow your future:

  • 401(k) plan with employer match and immediate vesting
  • Tuition reimbursement available
  • Student loan repayment assistance
  • Life, AD&D, and disability insurance

Wellbeing

Resources to help you thrive physically and emotionally:

  • Recognition rewards to support healthy habits
  • Free mental health coaching available 24/7
  • Family-planning support
  • Paid parental leave

Additional

Benefits that support what's important to you:

  • PTO: 160 hours to start, plus holidays and volunteer time off
  • Gift-matching program for charitable donations
  • Competitive pay
  • Company-sponsored events

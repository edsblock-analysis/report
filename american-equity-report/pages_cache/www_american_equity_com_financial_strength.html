What are financial ratings?

Ratings are more than numbers. They’re a measure of trust.

When you purchase an annuity from a company, you’re placing your future in their hands. Financial strength ratings help reassure contract owners that a company has the resources and resilience to deliver on its promises, even through economic downturns.   

Rating agencies assess a company’s ability to meet its guarantees and financial obligations. Each agency uses its own scale and criteria, but all share a focus on financial health, claims-paying ability, and business fundamentals to help consumers and financial professionals make informed decisions when selecting an annuity provider.

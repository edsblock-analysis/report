American Equity is a marketing/content site on Optimizely CMS with a Next.js front end. Because the DOM labels every component with a data-component name, block boundaries and their placement per template are read directly from the markup rather than guessed. All 74 URLs returned HTTP 200; 10 are legacy 301/308 redirects to canonical pages, leaving 64 distinct rendered pages across 11 templates.

What this report gives pre-sales: (2) the exact list of URLs analyzed with live links, (3) the 16 reusable blocks with their variations and complexity, (4) a per-template block↔variation mapping showing which blocks appear on each template and how many pages use each variation, (5) verified third-party integrations, (6) assumptions & scope boundaries, and (7) other technical observations. The heavier items are the Tabs block, the client-hydrated listings (Insights + form/document libraries), and third-party embeds that are preserved rather than rebuilt.
